Agile Resources

Tools, Books, and References for Your Agile Journey

Essential Reading

  • Agile Manifesto & Principles - The foundational document
  • Scrum Guide - The definitive Scrum framework
  • The Phoenix Project - DevOps and IT transformation
  • Team Topologies - Organizing teams for fast flow
  • Accelerate - The science of lean software and DevOps
  • User Story Mapping - Building better products

Frameworks & Methodologies

  • Scrum - Most popular Agile framework
  • Kanban - Visual workflow management
  • SAFe - Scaled Agile Framework
  • LeSS - Large-Scale Scrum
  • XP (Extreme Programming) - Engineering practices
  • DevOps - Development and Operations collaboration

Collaboration Tools

  • Jira - Project and issue tracking
  • Trello - Visual project management
  • Miro - Online whiteboard collaboration
  • Confluence - Team documentation and knowledge base
  • Slack - Team communication
  • Azure DevOps - End-to-end DevOps toolchain

Quality & Testing

  • Test-Driven Development (TDD) - Writing tests first
  • Behavior-Driven Development (BDD) - Specification by example
  • Continuous Integration - Frequent code integration
  • Continuous Delivery - Automated deployment pipeline
  • Definition of Done - Quality standards
  • Acceptance Criteria - Story completeness

Retrospective Techniques

  • Start, Stop, Continue - Simple retrospective format
  • 4Ls (Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed For)
  • Sailboat - Metaphorical retrospective
  • Mad, Sad, Glad - Emotional retrospective
  • Five Whys - Root cause analysis
  • Timeline Retrospective - Chronological review

Metrics & Measurement

  • Velocity - Team capacity measurement
  • Cycle Time - Time from start to finish
  • Lead Time - Customer request to delivery
  • Burndown Charts - Progress visualization
  • Cumulative Flow Diagrams - Work in progress
  • DORA Metrics - DevOps performance indicators

Communities & Learning

  • Agile Alliance - Global Agile community
  • Scrum.org - Professional Scrum training
  • Scrum Alliance - Certified Scrum training
  • LeSS Framework - Large-Scale Scrum community
  • Agile Coaching Institute - Coach development
  • Ministry of Testing - Testing community

Leadership & Culture

  • Servant Leadership - Leading by serving
  • Psychological Safety - Team effectiveness
  • Growth Mindset - Continuous learning culture
  • Radical Candor - Caring personally while challenging directly
  • Turn the Ship Around! - Leader-leader model
  • The Five Dysfunctions of a Team - Team health
