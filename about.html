About The Living Agilist

A Journey of Continuous Learning and Transformation

Living Agile, Not Just Practicing It

For nearly two decades, I've been immersed in the world of Agile—not as a set of ceremonies to check off, but as a living philosophy of continuous improvement, adaptation, and human-centered collaboration.

My journey began before I even knew what "Agile" meant. I was a reluctant entrepreneur, a determined QA engineer, and eventually, a passionate advocate for better ways of working. Along the way, I've learned that the most profound transformations happen not in boardrooms or training sessions, but in the daily interactions between people trying to solve real problems together.

My Philosophy

I believe that Agile is fundamentally about three things:

Human Connection: The best solutions emerge when diverse perspectives collaborate with respect and trust. Agile is first and foremost about people—their growth, their collaboration, and their shared success.

Built-In Quality: Quality isn't something you test in at the end; it's woven into every conversation, every decision, and every line of code from the very beginning. A well-executed waterfall project with strong quality practices can outperform a poorly implemented Agile approach—agility without quality is just chaos.

Organic Growth: Like sedimentary rock forming layer by layer, true Agile transformation is steady, natural, and strengthened over time. Quick fixes and forced changes rarely last. Sustainable change comes from patience, learning, and continuous adaptation.

What I Do

Throughout my career, I've worn many hats:

  • Product Owner & Scrum Master (before I knew those titles existed)
  • QA Champion building quality practices from the ground up
  • Agile Coach helping teams discover their own path to effectiveness
  • Transformation Leader guiding organizations through meaningful change
  • Continuous Learner always seeking new insights and perspectives

Today, I dedicate my time to helping individuals and organizations not just adopt Agile practices, but truly embrace the Agile mindset. I work with teams to build quality into their DNA, develop servant leaders who empower rather than control, and create cultures where continuous improvement is a natural way of life.

Why "The Living Agilist"?

The name reflects my belief that Agile is not a destination but a journey—a living, breathing practice that evolves with each team, each project, and each lesson learned. Like a living organism, it adapts, grows, and responds to its environment.

I'm not here to prescribe rigid frameworks or one-size-fits-all solutions. Instead, I share stories, insights, and lessons learned from nearly twenty years in the trenches. I celebrate both successes and failures, because both teach us something valuable.

Let's Connect

Whether you're just beginning your Agile journey or you're a seasoned practitioner looking for fresh perspectives, I'm here to learn alongside you. This space is as much about listening and learning as it is about sharing.

I invite you to explore the blog, engage with the content, and most importantly, share your own experiences. After all, the best learning happens when we're willing to be both teachers and students.
